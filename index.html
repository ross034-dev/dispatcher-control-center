Calculators

Fast load math for negotiation and planning.

Loaded RPM

—
Rate ÷ loaded miles

All-in RPM

—
Rate ÷ (loaded + deadhead)

Deadhead %

—
DH ÷ total miles × 100

Dispatcher commission

—
Gross × commission %

Required rate

—
Total miles × target RPM

Revenue per day

—
Rate ÷ days tied up
